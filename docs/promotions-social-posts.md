# Cardtly Growth Promotions — Social Post Drafts

Five ready-to-paste posts for Instagram, Facebook, and (where noted)
LinkedIn / X. Designed to roll out in sequence over the life of the
promotion. Each post has:

- **Caption** — paste straight into the platform
- **Hook** — the first line (the only line shown in feed previews)
- **Visual direction** — what the accompanying image/video should be
- **CTA** — the action you want the reader to take
- **Hashtags** — tested combination for SA reach
- **Cross-platform notes** — variations for LinkedIn, X, Threads

> **House rules.** No em dashes in copy. No "yebo" / forced slang. SA
> Rand for prices. Voice: confident, plain, slightly playful — same
> energy as the `/promotions` page itself.

---

## When to post each one

| # | Post | When to publish |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch** | Day 0 — the day you flip `/promotions` public |
| 2 | **Scarcity push** | When ~50 of the 100 founder slots are claimed |
| 3 | **Referral nudge** | ~10 days into the promo, or when growth slows |
| 4 | **Tier 1 closing** | When 90+ slots are claimed |
| 5 | **Tier 1 winners + Tier 2 live** | The day you record the lifetime winners |

If you don't hit a trigger (e.g. 50 slots never happens) just shift
the timing — the posts still read fine in sequence.

## Hashtag bank (use 5-10 per post)

matters is local.

`#SouthAfrica` `#ProudlySA` `#SmallBusinessSA` `#SouthAfricanBusiness`
`#JoziStartup` `#CapeTownStartup` `#EkasiBusiness` `#MzansiBusiness`
`#SAEntrepreneurs`

`#DigitalBusinessCard` `#NFCBusinessCard` `#NetworkingTools`
`#FreelancerLife` `#SideHustle` `#Entrepreneur` `#SmallBusiness`
`#StartupSA` `#SalesEnablement`

**Promo / draw specific:**
`#WinWithCardtly` `#CardtlyFounders` `#Giveaway` `#LifetimeAccess`
`#NoPurchaseNecessary`

`#Lotto` (regulated term, not legally a lottery), `#Lottery`,

## Anti-pattern checklist

Do not, in any of these posts:

- Promise the Tier 4 grand prize (we're keeping it mystery until
  5,000 paid)
- Imply that purchase is necessary (it's not — say so explicitly)
- Suggest the chance of winning depends on social activity
  (it doesn't anymore — we dropped the social entry mechanic)
- Quote a number ("87 left") that you haven't checked against the
  live counter at posting time
- Post a winner's full name, ID, photo, or address without their
  explicit written consent (POPIA — first name + city only by
  default)
- Use the word "lottery" or "raffle" (these have regulated meanings
  in SA law and we don't qualify)
- Run paid social ads targeting under-18s (eligibility starts at 18)
